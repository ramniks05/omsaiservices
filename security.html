Security Guards & Gunmen

Armed/Unarmed guards, gunmen, and bouncers for premises, banks, events, and VIP protection. Background-verified, uniformed, and supervised 24x7.

Overview

We deploy trained, disciplined, and verified personnel for access control, patrolling, CCTV monitoring, emergency response, and protocol enforcement. Each site receives supervisor checks, attendance tracking, and incident reporting.

Service Offerings

Static Guards
Gate control, frisking, visitor management, logbook, and asset protection.
Mobile Patrols

Gunmen
Licensed armed personnel for high-security sites and cash/asset movement.
Bouncers
